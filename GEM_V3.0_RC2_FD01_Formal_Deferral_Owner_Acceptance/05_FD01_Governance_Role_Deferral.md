## Against the Register's exact wording
- AC19 in the Register is "Approved — governance model is accepted; actual named role holders must be assigned before release" (status: Approved with modification).
- The *Owners & Governance* sheet states for every role: "actual named holder must be recorded before AC20 final release authorization."
- The Register's instruction sheet adds: "Before AC20, replace all TBD governance holders with actual names."

Consequently the deferral is a **postponement, not a satisfaction**: the acceptance condition is unmet and AC19 stays open. It also means the dedicated AC20 decision will need either the holders to be named, or an explicit owner decision about how that Register condition is treated. FD01 does not make that decision.
