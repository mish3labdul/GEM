## Visual Style
Rooted in high-end hospitality and modern spa aesthetics, the visual identity pairs raw **travertine** stonework with sleek, **minimalist** packaging. The presentation emphasizes material luxury, contrasting industrial pump dispensers and glass against deeply **tactile**, fibrous paper stocks.

## Composition
Framing relies on **architectural** geometry, staging subjects within rounded niches, on stepped stone ledges, and along precise horizontals. Layouts employ deliberate **asymmetric** balance, frequently offsetting product groupings against vast negative space and **sculptural** voids.

## Attitude
The visual tone is deeply **serene**, evoking the quiet exclusivity of a secluded luxury sanctuary. A sense of **restrained** opulence and **contemplative** calm replaces overt commercial energy with refined stillness.

## Colors
The palette is anchored by porous, sandy **beige** stone and creamy off-white packaging, punctuated by glowing **amber** glass and deep carbon **black** hardware. The resulting harmony is organic and strictly neutral, avoiding high chroma in favor of tonal warmth.

## Lighting
Strong, **raking** natural sunlight enters at low angles to cast elongated architectural shadows and crisp window mullion projections. This directional light carves out dramatic **chiaroscuro** contrast while bathing the travertine in a golden, **warm** daylight glow.

## Camera
Scenes are captured from eye-level and slightly elevated **tabletop** angles using medium to short-telephoto lenses. The rendering features edge-to-edge sharpness on typography and debossed details, framed by a **shallow** depth of field that softens architectural backgrounds into gentle **blur**.
