# Product naming approval, 18 October 2026

Status: OWNER APPROVED, ENGLISH NAMING ONLY. This local record is not a publication or production approval.

## Decision evidence

The owner was asked: “Do you approve these exact English names and the decision to omit a baseline collection name? Approval would cover naming only.” The question included the exact naming package below and explicitly excluded artwork layout and website navigation changes. The owner replied: “yes”. The owner subsequently instructed: “reflect on repo”. Date uses Asia/Riyadh.

This records the direct chat decision. It supersedes earlier working-name or pending-name status only for the entries below. It does not modify historical records or released V3.0 documents.

## Preserved identity and scope

GEM™ remains the master brand. Amenities & Packaging remains the approved descriptive B2B stream. The fixed tagline remains “Hospitality, in perfect proportion.” Product names remain Shampoo, Conditioner, Body Wash, Body Lotion and Hand Wash.

No new sub-brand, logo, palette or manufacturing claim is approved. Naming does not establish set contents, volumes, formulas, materials, quantities, price, MOQ, lead time, availability or terms. Co-branding placement and partner-mark precedence remain subject to their existing rules and approvals.

Arabic naming, legal/trademark clearance, supplier validation, artwork acceptance, commercial scope and publication remain separate decisions. B01/B02, D8, AC03/VAL-01 and other existing restrictions remain in force except that the specific English naming questions above are resolved. Website navigation alternatives were not approved through this decision.
