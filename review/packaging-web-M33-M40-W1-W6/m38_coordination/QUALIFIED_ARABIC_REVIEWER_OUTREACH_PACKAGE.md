## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

GEM™ has established an approved bilingual front-label design for five luxury hospitality personal-care products under Owner Decisions `GEM-PKG-OWNER-20261107-001` (English Ink visual concept), `GEM-PKG-OWNER-20261107-002` (Five locked Arabic product names), and `GEM-PKG-OWNER-20261107-003` (Bilingual Layout A: shared left-aligned axis at `x = 200 px`, Jost + Noto Sans Arabic).

While extensive machine-measured optical and HarfBuzz shaping validations were conducted during Milestones M15, M24a, and M30, **automated analysis cannot replace the human judgement of a native Arabic reader.** This package provides a structured, respectful, and zero-cost mechanism to solicit expert feedback from a qualified native Arabic typographer or brand designer.

## 4. Self-Contained Review Request Cover Brief

*(Draft text ready for project owner transmission via email or direct messaging)*

```markdown
Subject: Technical Review Request: Bilingual Arabic/English Luxury Packaging Typography (GEM Hospitality)

Dear [Reviewer Name],

We are developing bilingual (Arabic and English) front-label packaging artwork for GEM™, a minimalist luxury personal-care brand designed for premier hospitality environments in Saudi Arabia. 

We would deeply value your professional perspective as a native Arabic typography and design specialist to evaluate our typographic execution across five product labels:
• Shampoo — شامبو
• Conditioner — بلسم
• Body Wash — غسول الجسم
• Body Lotion — لوشن الجسم
• Hand Wash — غسول اليدين

### Scope of the Review:
Our automated geometric and font-engine checks verify that text paths and baselines align correctly, but software cannot evaluate natural reading comfort, optical balance, or cultural elegance. We are seeking your opinion on:
1. Naturalness and appropriateness of the Arabic terminology within contemporary luxury hospitality in the Gulf / Saudi Arabia.
2. Optical size, stroke weight, and visual hierarchy between the Latin (Jost) and Arabic (Noto Sans Arabic) typography.
3. Left-aligned (flush-left) Arabic composition sharing a common left axis with the English title and brand emblem.
4. Word spacing within two-word compounds (غسول الجسم, لوشن الجسم, غسول اليدين).
5. Legibility and dot differentiation at small physical package dimensions.

### Governance Notice:
Please note that the five product terms and the single-axis layout represent locked conceptual directions chosen by the brand owner. Your insights will directly inform the brand owner's executive review and will not be applied automatically. The attached images represent unreleased brand concept artwork; please treat them confidentially.

### Review Package Contents:
The attached package includes high-resolution 100% label renders, comparative multi-scale sheets (50% and 14%), simulated screen magnifications, and a concise 14-question review form (estimated completion time: 20–30 minutes).

If you are open to sharing your insights, please review the attached materials and return your thoughts using the response form by [Target Date: Owner to specify].

Warm regards,

[Owner / Creative Director Name]  
GEM™ Packaging & Brand Identity Program
```
