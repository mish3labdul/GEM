    Working with GEM

    Property and brand teams: tell us about the property and what it needs. Suppliers and manufacturers: tell us your formats, technical requirements and test evidence. We agree scope and terms with each partner.

    Properties and brands

    A Property Edition would place property information within the GEM system. Each identity keeps its own artwork and clearspace, and which brand leads is agreed with you.

    Suppliers and manufacturers

    Work would start from the correct master artwork and a confirmed specification. GEM issues artwork for one job, and it stays with that job.

    How a job reaches production

    Every job passes through brief, specification, artwork, proof, review and approval before production. We are still validating this standard.
