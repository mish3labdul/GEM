What this preview collects

Nothing. The enquiry form prepares a draft in your browser so you can copy it or open it in your email app. It does not send, store or log what you type, and this site sets no cookies and loads no analytics or third-party services.

Sending an enquiry

If you choose “Open in your email app”, the draft opens in your own email program, addressed to GEM. Nothing leaves your device unless you send it from there. The details you send (your name, email address, organisation or property, and message) will be used only to reply to your enquiry. GEM’s approved notice will say how long enquiries are kept and how to ask for them to be corrected or deleted.
