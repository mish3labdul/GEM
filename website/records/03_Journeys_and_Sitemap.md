# 03 · Visitor journeys and sitemap (proposed)

## Journeys

**Prospective client** (owner or developer, operations, procurement, brand and design teams)
1. Understand in one screen what GEM is: a hospitality supplier and partner, not an operator. *(Part A 12)*
2. See what GEM provides today: the Amenities & Packaging stream, honestly marked as in development. *(Part A 16, 60–62; Part D)*
3. Recognise their own role and what GEM offers it. *(Part A 14–15)*
4. Next step: prepare an enquiry. **Truthful limit:** no receiving inbox, email address or phone number is documented, so the form prepares the enquiry and says plainly that it has not been sent.

**Partner company** (property brand, supplier, manufacturer, fabricator)
1. Understand how co-branding works: side by side, own artwork, precedence by agreement. *(Part A 31; Part D 12)*
2. Understand how production work flows: the 14-step release sequence; GEM artwork used only for the issued job. *(Part C 5; Part B 5, Y06)*
3. Next step: the same enquiry form with "Becoming a partner".

**Evidence gap for both:** no client names, projects, case studies or testimonials exist. The page says so in one line instead of inventing them.
